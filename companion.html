Randy: RydeMe method

Reported method; no verified Series S reproduction here. Use game prompts and test one car.

  1. Meet partner; find and scan wanted car.
  2. Request RydeMe to Randy's garage first.
  3. Clone key; enter with partner; choose Wait In via D-pad Right menu.
  4. Meet RydeMe on accessible road; board.
  5. Choose Follow Me; hold LT and select Skip Journey ($100).
  6. Wait for partner, then deliver carefully.

Blocked driveway or non-starting car? No guaranteed workaround. Choose another target.

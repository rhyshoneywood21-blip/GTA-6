## Useful new findings
- Pawn-shop Smash 'n' Grabs reportedly open after Coffee Break in Chapter 2, jewelry shops after Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs in Chapter 3. There are 15 pawn and 20 jewelry targets; 34 of 35 count for 100%. Reported gross ranges vary from $1k–25k by store. https://game-checklists.com/gta6/smash-n-grabs-checklist/ and https://www.vicecityhustle.com/guides/gta-6-smash-and-grab-locations
- Kenton: Island Dreams unlocks early dive markers; sell completed finds to Kenton. Later locations require story progression, including Maritime Dispute. The 5-of-7 sets threshold does not remove the separate 33 underwater-discovery requirement. Source: today's PowerPyx dive walkthrough above.
- Safe code counts differ: trackers list 26 known fixed codes, a separate guide lists 43 combinations including observed variable bank codes and keypads. Do not treat all as universal. Check clues and actual in-game prompt.
- 91 story missions across five chapters is the common count; alternate sites count more by including optional sequences. Avoid mission lists if you want no spoilers.

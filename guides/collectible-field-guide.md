## Best collection habits
- Scan each new vehicle and wildlife species encountered naturally.
- Keep a separate check of full card sets; a card count alone does not determine completion.
- Kenton's Island Dreams introduces treasure diving. Use matching map and detector clues.
- Complete early balcony meditation if offered, because the all-meditations achievement has possible missable reports.
- If aiming only at 100%, stop a category when the in-game threshold is reached.
- If pursuing all achievements, consult the separate trophy guide for requirements beyond 100%.

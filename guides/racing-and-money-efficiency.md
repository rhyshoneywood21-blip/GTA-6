# Low-risk earnings and route testing
## Racing alternative to robberies
Gellhorn Raceway (Chapter 2): introductory lap with Beau; reported first-place payouts $2,500 basic/short and up to $3,750 long/rallycross. Rental reported $500; net $2,000 on a $2,500 win. Repeat payouts not independently confirmed.
Later: Beach Blast (Ocean Beach) and Big Mud (Thrillbilly) reported max $6,000. Rialto Run $5,000; Muck Scramble $4,000. Look at the in-game race card for rental cost, vehicle restrictions, laps and payout. The easiest short race may beat the highest nominal reward per minute.
Source: https://cutscenewire.com/games/gta-6/side-content/gta-6-all-40-unique-race-locations-and-how-to-unlock-them/ ; https://gtaviceguides.com/guides/gta-6-repeatable-money-methods/
## Income comparisons
Moses reported payouts vary considerably across sources ($125–825 vs $1k+), with roughly two-minute cooldown. Use own observed amounts, not a promise. Chapter 3 Randy is finite list progression, not infinite money. Jewelry reported $10–25k per job; vaults late Ch4 $65–205k gross. No confirmed passive-income business system found in this pass.
Source: https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-money-guide-how-to-get-money-fast ; https://gtaviceguides.com/guides/gta-6-repeatable-money-methods/
## Measurement
Net = money actually received minus travel, repair, rental and consumables. Count all elapsed real minutes including fencing, failed runs and waiting. Net/hour = 60 × net/minutes. Log separately for each player and save. A $25k run taking ten minutes beats a $40k run taking twenty minutes. Do not extrapolate one unusually successful run into a sustainable hourly rate.
Use [run log template](../data/run-log.csv) for at least three attempts per method. Prioritize ease and consistency over maximum theoretical earnings.

# Easier Xbox gameplay
For both players, use the actual Xbox prompts rather than PlayStation equivalents.
## Accessibility
Accessible Combat, Reduce Vehicle Damage, Tap/Hold Assist: inspect settings and enable individually as preferred. No verified evidence here that these affect cash payouts; compare before/after on same activity if concerned. Prefer less demanding hold inputs, aiming assistance, readable HUD and subtitles when available.
## Partner and driving
Reported auto-drive sequence: Settings > Gameplay > Auto Drive > Unrestricted, mark destination, use driving menu. Confirm prompts in-game. Partner can help with carrying and selling loot; check actual menu availability and wait for sale confirmation. Never assume an exploit works just because a partner is present.

## Efficient route
Finish nearby required categories together, use the exact provider pin, mark found only after the game confirms the item. When doing Kenton sets, track the underwater-discovery counter separately. Prioritize complete card sets over scattered cards if the goal is 100%. For an all-achievements run, compare the trophy roadmap with the completion checklist.
## Map audit
Existing source files have 1,384 collectible-category pins, 1,067 activity pins and 6,642 merged location records overall. They are source records, not independently verified positions. Source counts such as 592 card pins vs 480 cards may include duplicates, alternate variants or misclassified entries; 11 meditation pins do not prove all 18 completions are mapped. Keep the game's own Progress counter authoritative.

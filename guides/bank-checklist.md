## Unlock and completion
Small banks: Chapter **3**, Credit Check. Large vaults: late Chapter 4, That's Where the Money Is… . Map > Robberies > Bank Jobs. Small completion requires both drawer and safe; large adds vault. Bright green marker indicates completion. Marked bills need the game's dye pack jammer and fencing. All 22 for completion; five large vaults for Maximum Withdrawal.

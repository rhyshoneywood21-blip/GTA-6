|---|---|---|
| Brian | Key Lento | Ch1 Lab Fees; lowest rates |
| Donnie | Key Lento | Ch1 Clean Up After Donnie; better narcotics than Brian |

## Bank jobs
14 small + 8 large = 22. Small banks after Credit Check; vaults near end Ch4 after That's Where The Money Is… (Wait at the Cafe). Use Map > Robberies > Bank Jobs. Five large vaults reportedly unlock Maximum Withdrawal achievement. All 22 for completion checklist. Repeat availability and cooldown must be checked in-game.
| Large branch | Reported max |
|---|---:|
| Downtown Lombank | $205,000 |
| Ocean Beach Lombank (high payout) | $205,000 |
| Airport VCIA Fleeca | $150,000 |
| Downtown Fleeca | $150,000 |
| Ocean Beach Shorefront | $140,000 |
| Ocean Beach Fleeca | $135,000 |
| Ocean Beach Lombank (other branch) | $135,000 |
| Tequesta Fleeca | $110,000 |
These are gross potential maxima, not guaranteed cash, and some require fencing. Distinguish two Ocean Beach Lombanks on the actual map.

## Compare routes
Log sale proceeds minus travel, ammunition and repairs. Divide by real minutes spent. If a route requires waiting for respawn, count that time. Prefer reliable unlocked tasks to low-evidence glitches.

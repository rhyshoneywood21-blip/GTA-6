# Equipment, phone and hidden gameplay tools
Research checked 26 November 2026. Contextual controller prompts override this guide.
## All 12 equipment entries
| Equipment | In-game function | Unlock information |
|---|---|---|
| Binoculars | Observe wildlife and objects | Gone Fishing, Ch1 |
| Flashlight | Visibility in dark areas | Gone Fishing, Ch1 |
| Duffel bag | Store loot | Lab Fees, Ch1 |
| Headphones | Listening | Exception to story unlocks; see equipment guide |
| Pry bar | Contextual interaction with containers | Story unlock; check progress |
| Masks | Disguise | Story unlock; not immunity to recognition |
| Metal detector | Finds buried and underwater treasure | Island Dreams, Ch1 |
| Slim Jim | Vehicle entry | Story unlock |
| Snap Gun | Lock interaction | Story unlock |
| Cut-off tool | Contextual cutting | Story unlock |
| Key Cloner | Keyless vehicle access | Clonin' Keys, Makin' Gs, Ch3 |
| Dye Pack Jammer | In-game marked-bill activity tool | Credit Check, Ch3 |
Scuba gear is introduced through Island Dreams; not part of the twelve Progress equipment entries.

## Phone shortcuts
| App | Use |
|---|---|
| WAiNK | Scan car model, tracker and security; scan collectibles when prompted |
| RydeMe | Ride to accessible destination; skip journey for additional charge |
| BuckMe | Checking, savings and ATM finder |
| Scooter Bros | Deliver a registered personal vehicle |
| Vice City Real Estate | Garage purchase/availability |
| WhatUp / Contacts | Check texts, meet partner, obtain mission leads |
| Fitness | Workout window and nutrition/stat tracking |
| Snapmatic | Social posts; some lead to side content |
| Camera / Gallery | Photos and in-game clues |
| Weather | County weather and forecasts |
| On Demand | Audio content |
| News / Settings | In-game headlines and customization |
Reported practical shortcuts: use Focus (both stick clicks on default) to highlight contextual clues; hold D-pad Right for partner menu; hold D-pad Left for vehicle controls; dark blue map buildings may be enterable. Inspect on-screen options before relying on any combo.
## Partner and character care
Respond to partner messages, use available meet-up commands and try the driver/gunner swap when available. Fitness app tracks workout timing; do not confuse temporary strength changes with permanent maximum-stat upgrades. Use safehouse rest and in-game healing prompts as required.

# Money and progression
## Chapter priorities
- Chapter 2: Moses, vehicle registration and fencing; compare available buyers.
- Chapter 3: Randy's Wish List; high-value cars, condition bonus. Tyrone for narcotics, Yasmani for jewelry and marked bills (reported favorable rates).
- Chapter 3: Credit Check opens small bank jobs. Late Chapter 4: That's Where the Money Is… opens large vault jobs. See [all 22 targets](bank-checklist.md).

## Sustainable routine
Pick a cluster of unlocked activities, check bag/vehicle storage, use partner commands when available, clear wanted status, sell at best unlocked fence, deposit spending cash into ordinary checking. Track net money after travel, ammunition and damage; avoid repeating unavailable activities.
Family Savings is reported deposit-only; verify UI before depositing funds needed for equipment.

## Protect your cash
In-game Help screenshots report a $20,000 pocket cash limit for each character. Death/arrest can remove carried cash and unowned weapons, charge a maintenance fee and cause loss of carried bags. Use ATM deposits and keep a small amount for day-to-day costs. Recover owned weapons through the personal vehicle trunk or weapon locker when available.

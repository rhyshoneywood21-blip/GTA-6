# Play tonight — Xbox Series S
Updated 26 Nov 2026. Story mode. No major story spoilers.
## Five-minute setup (each player)
Settings > Gameplay / Accessibility: enable Accessible Combat, Reduced Vehicle Damage, Drive-By Assist and adjust Tap Assist and Hold Assist as preferred. Set Auto Drive to Unrestricted if driving is difficult. Tune aim sensitivity/deadzones and subtitles individually. Rockstar documents these features, but we found no official confirmation of a payout multiplier or penalty.
Official: https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/5g5CmDTne3WcpK7nsWsMR8/grand-theft-auto-vi-accessibility-features
## What to do at your current chapter
- 1: progress story, unlock transport; sell available loot to Donnie rather than Brian when appropriate. Keep separate manual saves.
- 2: unlock Moses; consider using his one-time free registration on a useful vehicle rather than a disposable car. Collect the three Port Gellhorn safe rewards. Start stat quests.
- 3: complete Clonin' Keys, Makin' Gs; open Randy list in map. Hunt Bayside dealership, Southside showrooms, rich driveways. Use partner delivery on one car before repeating. Sell goods to Tyrone/Yasmani.
- 4: Photo/Shoot and Dale Mamba for advanced-tracker registration; complete bank-vault unlock mission. Prioritize large vaults over low-value shops when available.
- Post-story: complete outstanding map objectives and collectibles. Cheats disable saving/achievements for the session, so not a permanent money strategy.
## 30-minute Chapter 3 route
1. Manual save. Check outstanding Randy targets.
2. Start Bayside: check Status Auto Imports and dealership west of Dynamix Fitness (verify map). Scan parked vehicles, select only needed model.
3. Use partner delivery or drive normally. Record actual payout and trip time.
4. Check Southside hexagonal showroom cluster for remaining models.
5. If targets remain, check Peacock Estates or ask Randy for a marker.
6. Sell any existing loot to appropriate fence; bank cash. Stop when a location stops producing new target cars.
## Travel shortcuts
RydeMe or Paradise Taxi: choose reachable destination, board, hold LT to focus on driver and press Y when Skip Journey appears. $100 additional charge. If gated pickup fails, move to public road; taxi may be an alternative. If services stop responding, save and fully close/reopen game (community workaround, not guaranteed).

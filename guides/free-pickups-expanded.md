## Keep your finds
- Check the weapon locker or personal vehicle trunk after collecting a unique weapon.
- Open the variants tab to find registered variants.
- Some variants cannot be modified. Confirm that your item is registered before leaving.
See the free weapon collection route for Pelican Valley, Permit Keys and Griffin bunker.

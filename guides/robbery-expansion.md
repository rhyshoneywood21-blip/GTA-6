# Money: all robbery categories and chapter priorities
Research checked 26 November 2026. In-game activities only. No warehouse exploit. Amounts are reported gross ranges, not guaranteed payouts or repeat availability.
## Pick a job by chapter
| Chapter | Priority | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Story jobs, Kenton treasure diving | Unlock tools, keep money in checking once available |
| 2 | Kolt operations, Bret hunting, Gellhorn Raceway, Moses | Variety without requiring late-game vaults |
| 3 | Randy, jewelry/pawn jobs, Payday Loans, small banks | Higher-paying jobs and better fences |
| 4 onward | Large bank vaults, remaining operations and collection goals | Highest reported gross payouts |
Randy is a finite quest, not unlimited income. Normal racing is a low-combat alternative; sources disagree about repeat payouts so test before grinding. Deposit earned cash into checking; Family Savings is not a normal spending account.

## Lower-stress play
Set accessible combat, reduced damage, simplified minigames and aim assistance if desired. For a first attempt choose one available smaller job; if repeatedly failing, switch to racing, exploration, car hunting or story progression. Use partner menu for legitimate assistance. Complete all marked objectives and check green completion status.

# Collectibles and useful unlocks
Counts from in-game checklist guides. No full story walkthroughs. Cheats unlock after story completion, disable saving and achievements in the session; not permanent progression methods.
| Goal | Reward / effect reported |
|---|---|
| Complete 3 card sets | Don't Touch My Deck achievement |
| Complete 15 of 18 card sets | 100% checklist |
| 24 Vice City Flamingos cards | Bubblegum filter |
| Destroy 50 Cocaine Flamingos | Inflatable Flamingo cheat |
| 10 collectible watches | Move Time Forward |
| 20 collectible watches | Pause Time of Day |
| Scan all space debris | Powdered Sugar filter |
| Sell $250,000 collectibles | Pixel Art filter |
| 50 meditations | Fast Forward filter |
| 100 fish | Fisheye filter |
| 25 gang operations with partner | Pinhole filter |
| 50 activities with partner | Videotape filter |
Cards: color variants count separately, duplicates do not increase unique count. Reportedly selling a discovered card retains the collection entry; check game before selling rare items. For three-set achievement, smaller sets Backyard Wrestling (12), Delights Dancers (18), and a 24-card set total 54. Some guides disagree on sizes. Vicki at Starlet Motel may be missable after Chapter 2; collect early if available. Do not interpret 592 source card markers as 592 unique cards. 
For flamingos, shoot the in-game lawn ornament to reveal the package. For WAiNK scan notifications, use phone scanner on foot. Complete nearby activities together, but use game counters for actual progress.

# Additional income and low-stress routes
## Pilot Pete
Chapter 4, Jason, after Pool Day / Pete contact. Five linked optional jobs, not an infinite repeatable farm. Complete earlier Last Mile and Backseat Pilot tasks, then wait for the delivery text. Southpoint Airfield and Avery Allen & Co. each reportedly pay $20,000 via BuckMe if completed before their in-game deadlines (20:00 and 14:00 respectively). The fifth job follows after a few in-game days and reportedly pays $20,000 for seven packages before 08:00. Earlier Last Mile payout reported $12,000. Follow map prompts, not a guessed route. 
Sources: https://www.gta6heists.com/missions/petes-deliveries ; https://completegta6.com/vehicles/gta-6-pilot-pete-missions-how-to-start-deliveries/ ; https://www.gtacorner.com/side-missions/pilot-pete
## Kenton correction
Published sources report 41 treasures across seven sets and $200 per treasure (about $8,200 for all 41 at that rate, excluding other rewards). Do not promise tens of thousands solely from those sales. Island Dreams provides scuba and detector. Use pink chest map markers, scan seabed, watch oxygen, sell at Kenton in Key Lento; progress story and wait for new messages. Later dives after Maritime Dispute. 5 sets for collection requirement; all 33 underwater discoveries separately.
Sources: https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-all-kenton-sets-locations-search-the-dive-sites/ ; https://www.keengamer.com/articles/guides/gta-6-all-7-kentons-treasure-set-locations-and-rewards/ ; https://gta6.house/library/characters/kenton
## Suggested sessions (not measured or guaranteed repeatable)
- Low stress: collect nearby free gear, treasure dives, race or derby, then turn in rewards. Derby reported $3,800–$6,300 per win before rental/repairs.
- Midgame: active jewelry jobs and small banks, then best fence. Check actual map gross and whether the job has reset. 
- Late game: complete Pete's delivery chain for finite income, then available vaults. Downtown/Ocean Beach $205k upper bounds remain gross estimates.
- Glitch session: separate manual save, try a reported exploit once, compare bank balance; don't grind until payout is demonstrated.

# Vehicle finding — fixed spawns, random sightings and ownership
Research checked 26 November 2026.
## Use the correct source for the job
- [GTABase fixed spawns — 162 mapped entries](https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/map/?feature=vehicle-spawn): specific parked vehicles and aircraft.
- [GTA 6 Life vehicle map](https://gta6-life.com/en/tools/interactive-map/vehicles/): broader vehicle locations; map markers are not distinct models.
- [Randy list map](https://gta6gps.com/map/game/randys-wish-list): targeted list cars.
- [Cars for GTA6 & GTA5](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.codevsit.carcompanion.gta): external player sightings, presets and model comparison. No public export verified.
- [Open GTA 6 Map](https://fbritoferreira.github.io/gta6/): maintained embeddable map, see data attribution.
## Classify every sighting
Fixed: a source describes a particular vehicle at a particular spot. Random: possible models vary by traffic, weather, time or reload. Mission-only: depends on progression or marker. Player sighting: observed once, not proof of fixed spawn. Unknown: insufficient evidence.
When adding a sighting save model, landmark, map URL, date, build, chapter, time, conditions and repeat count in the local spawn journal. A confirmed fixed spawn should be repeatable in the same conditions. Avoid combining GTA5 and GTA6 records.
## Efficient car hunt
For Randy use Bayside dealership, Southside showroom cluster, Vespucci/Casanova driveway alternatives and Peacock Estates; scan before selecting. For permanent ownership check tracker, garage space and registration prerequisites. Maintain separate favorites/ownership from the Wish List.
## Wyman classics (Ultimate Edition)
15 wreck sites are mapped. The quest opens in Chapter 2 according to guides. Scan wrecks; Wyman restores cars for fees after waiting. Sources disagree about total cars and shared wreck counts; do not assume all are available in standard edition. [Wyman map](https://www.gtabase.com/fr/gta-6/carte/?feature=wyman-car); [restore guide](https://gtatown.com/garage/wyman).
## Data reuse
GTA Town creator data page advertises CC BY 4.0 exports, but the separate vehicle licence describes key-based conditions. No bulk vehicle import until scope is clarified. https://gtatown.com/creators/data ; https://gtatown.com/licence/data

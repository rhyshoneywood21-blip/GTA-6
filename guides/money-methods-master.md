# Money methods — low-stress and high-return
26 November 2026. Story mode, Xbox. No warehouse farming. All payouts are reported in-game dollars and not guaranteed net profits.
| Method | Availability | Reported return | Repeatability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Treasure dives with Kenton | Chapter 1, Island Dreams | Tens of thousands across finds | Finite discovery rewards; not unlimited |
| Bret hunting requests | Chapter 2, after Coffee Break, Jason only | $500 to several thousand | Check active requests |
| Ambrosia demolition derby | Once unlocked | $3,800–6,300 per win | Reported repeatable; subtract rental |
| Gellhorn Raceway | Chapter 2 | $2,500–3,750 per win | Verify repeat payouts |
| Other race events | Progress dependent | Up to $6,000 reported | Check event card |
| Ordinary illegal operations | Progress dependent | $1,000–15,000 typical reported range; exceptional sites higher | Respawn reported; key valuables may be one-time |
| Pawn/jewelry jobs | Chapter 2/3 | Varies by location; $1,000–25,000 reports | Check availability |
| Small banks | Chapter 3 | $5,000–45,000 gross | 14 targets; check cooldown |
| Randy | Chapter 3 onward | Vehicle-dependent | Finite objective list |
| Large vaults | Late Chapter 4 | $65,000–205,000 gross | Eight targets; check actual availability |
## Low-stress priority
For easier play use accessible combat and reduced vehicle damage, then favor treasure discoveries, races and active hunting tasks rather than forcing difficult raids. Complete one run, compare actual net receipts and duration. Derby at Ambrosia Raceway is a promising repeatable alternative; rental reduces profit. For hunting, check Bret's current requests and the actual game quality rating before selling. 
## Higher-return route
Chapter 3: complete available small banks and jewelry jobs, then use the best unlocked fences. Chapter 4: check vault availability, prioritize Downtown/Ocean Beach high-value branches. Use the existing [bank list](bank-checklist.md) and [fence guide](fences-and-banks.md). The listed map clusters are not timed optimal routes.
## Important correction
A Nov 22 report distinguishes normal gang respawns from unique key valuables: those one-off rewards reportedly do not return. Do not estimate repeat income using first-clear total. The separate death-reset candidate needs a test after the patch. Record gross received, expenses, travel/fence time and failures. No promised hourly rate.

# Free, hidden and unique weapon locations
Scope revised by user: keep pickup locations, unique finds, useful unlocks and equipment tips. Detailed weapon statistics and purchase catalogues are archived research, not required app content. Use Ammu-Nation for purchase comparisons.
Checked 26 November 2026. All weapons and numbers refer only to GTA VI.
## What the game tracks
66 standard weapons (53 guns, 9 melee, 4 explosives), 130 unique variants, 196 total. Only 50 of the standard items are needed for 100%. Collecting a variant is not a substitute for the game's standard-discovery counter. Use Progress > Weapons to confirm.
Source: https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-all-weapons/
The [weapon research catalogue](../data/weapons-catalogue.json) contains 51 cross-guide gun names and 16 additional candidate labels, not a confirmed complete roster. The [128 map pickups](../data/weapon-pickups.json) preserve provider pins and notes; many entries are variants or generic weapon labels. Do not merge items merely because they have similar names.
## Useful weapon choices
- Budget purchase: Nipper .38 ($550 reported); K17 ($975 reported). ES9 may already be owned.
- Budget rifle: KZ-59 ($1,250 reported). Several stat sources disagree, so compare your own weapon menu.
- Free pickups: use [the existing free weapon route](free-weapon-route.md) before paying for a duplicate.
- Purchased and found weapons can be retrieved from a gun-store locker or vehicle trunk, where available. Confirm ownership in Progress and test after saving; avoid abandoning an item before it is registered.
- Do not buy unique cosmetics solely for 100%; they do not count toward the 50 standard discoveries.
## Gun stores
24 stores documented by PowerPyx, with an alternate count of 26 franchises in other guides. Map county distribution: Mariana 5; Vice-Dale 14; Kelly 3; Leonard 1; Lummox 1. Store selection depends on stock tier: Limited, Standard, Hunting, Expanded. Expanded stores reportedly stock heavier weapons and sniper rifles; inspect map hover and actual inventory. Unique store variants may be exclusive.

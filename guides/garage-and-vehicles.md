# Garage ownership and practical vehicles
## Unlocks
Daily Driver (Chapter 2) opens real-estate purchases. Register vehicles to retain them; merely parking a stolen vehicle does not guarantee permanent ownership. A free slot is needed. One free Moses registration: prioritize useful cargo capacity or a car you genuinely intend to keep.

## Practical recommendation
Bravado Bison: six cargo slots, reported $9k registration avoided using Moses. A motorcycle may be easier to navigate through traffic, but low cargo capacity. For fast cars use the story's free Company Cars reward or registered free finds, rather than paying early just for speed. Check whether advanced trackers can be registered before attempting to keep supercars.

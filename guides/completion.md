## Save time
Scan unfamiliar cars, aircraft and boats as you encounter them. Study animals with binoculars instead of hunting every animal. Complete optional tasks as you go, especially those that introduce mechanics. Do not pursue all collectibles when a partial target suffices unless you want a full collection. The separate achievement list may impose extra requirements.

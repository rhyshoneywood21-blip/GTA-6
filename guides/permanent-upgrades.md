# Permanent character upgrades
Prioritize these before difficult missions. Permanent maximum stats differ from temporary workout/food boosts. Check each character's stats before and after; sources disagree on exact amounts and whether individual completion is required.
| Quest | Benefit reported | Starting cue |
|---|---|---|
| Alien encounter | Health, stamina, focus | Chapter 2 onward, alone, road NW of Starlet Motel after 2 AM; flashing vehicle / trail |
| Circles of Hell | Health | North Port Gellhorn, alone, midnight–4 AM; red-lit garage, two metalheads |
| Mushroom Trip | Stamina | Construction site southeast shore Lake Tallaloko, north of Millport; ??? interaction |
| Kyle's mutated strawberry | Stamina | Pilot Wanted flyer, Starlet Motel; finish Collect Chemicals for Kyle and accept berry |
| Final Flush | Stamina | Corsair Key public men's restroom; scan #NOFLUSHERS QR with in-game WAiNK; follow texts |
| Shooting range gold medals | Focus | Griffin / Rodgers Ranch shooting ranges; check medal requirements |
Other reported leads: Russo bunker brainwashing and iguana hunt (focus). Not verified here. Some guides disagree on strawberry permanence, exact quest locations and stat changes.
